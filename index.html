Publication


LOCOEYE – Detection of Railway Signal Lights and Pedestrians on Tracks using YOLOv8

Publication Details

  • Journal: Grenze International Journal of Engineering and Technology (GIJET)
  • Volume: 12 | Issue: 1 | Year: 2026
  • Grenze ID: 01.GIJET.12.1.389

The research was presented at NCCT2025 and published in the Grenze International Journal of Engineering and Technology (GIJET), January 2026 issue.

View Publication

Overview

Railway safety remains a critical challenge, particularly in preventing accidents caused by pedestrian intrusions, animals, and signal misinterpretation.
Our research introduces an AI-powered real-time railway monitoring system built using YOLOv8 for high-accuracy object detection.

  • Custom railway dataset collection and annotation
  • Data augmentation for robust generalization
  • Real-time video processing using OpenCV
  • ROI filtering & Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS)
  • Achieved 94.5% mAP with optimized inference speed
  • Integrated automated alert mechanism for rapid response
This system is designed to enhance situational awareness and contribute toward safer railway infrastructure using AI-driven automation.

Acknowledgment

This research was conducted at the Computer Science and Engineering Department, Gopalan College of Engineering and Management, under the guidance of Dr. Swathi Y, along with my project team.
